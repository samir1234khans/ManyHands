# ManyHands Code of Conduct

ManyHands exists so people with different skills, backgrounds, locations, and levels of experience can build useful things together. Participation requires behavior that makes collaboration safer and more possible.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and work, not a person’s worth or identity.
- Make room for newcomers and explain hidden context.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and give credit generously.
- Protect private information and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Disagree with evidence and curiosity rather than ridicule.
- Use project spaces for collaboration, not harassment, recruitment spam, or personal promotion.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexualized attention, doxxing, stalking, intimidation, sustained disruption, impersonation, malicious code, spam, and retaliation against a reporter are not allowed.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces, ManyHands community spaces, official events, and public conduct while representing the project.

## Reporting

Do not create a public issue when a report contains private, identifying, or safety-sensitive information. Contact the lead maintainer privately through the contact methods on their GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be handled with discretion. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised, but information will be limited to people who need it to respond.

## Enforcement

Responses may include clarification, a warning, content removal, temporary restrictions, removal from a project, revoked access, or a permanent ban. Severity, pattern, impact, safety, and willingness to repair will be considered.

Maintainers and moderators must not investigate a report in which they are materially involved. When no uninvolved maintainer exists, the project should seek a trusted external reviewer.

## No retaliation

Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report or participates in a review is itself a violation.
