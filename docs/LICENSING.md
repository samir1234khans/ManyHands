## Why AGPL

ManyHands is primarily a networked application. The project wants people to fork, modify, host, and improve it, while ensuring that users of modified networked versions can receive the corresponding source code. That keeps meaningful deployed improvements available to the commons instead of allowing the community’s work to disappear behind a hosted service.

## Practical meaning

You may use, study, modify, distribute, and host ManyHands, subject to the license. When a modified version is made available to users over a network, those users must be offered the corresponding source under the AGPL terms.

This is a practical summary, not a replacement for the license. The canonical terms are published by the Free Software Foundation at <https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html>.
