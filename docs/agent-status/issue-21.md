## Decisions that must not be reversed

- Do not claim that no competitor exists.
- Stars, search results, and opinions are not proof of demand.
- Distinguish discovery, formation, contribution handoff, progress, funding, and stewardship rather than collapsing them into one market category.
- Research may recommend normative changes but does not silently rewrite the product contract.
