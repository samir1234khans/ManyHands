# ManyHands research

Research in this directory is dated evidence, not a replacement for the constitution, product contract, domain model, or accepted Architecture Decision Records.

Each brief should state:

- the question and decision it informs;
- the research date;
- methodology and source limitations;
- current product-status claims with primary-source links;
- evidence for and against the working hypothesis;
- uncertainty and follow-up questions;
- recommended product implications rather than silent contract changes.

## Briefs

- [`2026-08-adjacent-platforms.md`](2026-08-adjacent-platforms.md) — adjacent open-source discovery, onboarding, coordination, health, mentorship, and funding products.

## Research integrity

Do not claim “no competitor exists.” A missing feature on one website, an inactive search result, a podcast opinion, or a repository star count is not proof of demand.

Research pull requests may recommend changes. Normative product changes require their own issue and review.
